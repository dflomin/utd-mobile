# Potions

- Potions are mostly permanent numeric stat investment.
- Drop randomly from enemies, with elite/special loot mobs and bosses having higher odds.
- Common potions may be +1% style bonuses.
- Rare variants may be +5% or stronger.
- Potions generally do not add mechanics/effects.
- Effect-changing modifiers are reserved for items.
- Potion investment is not recoverable when selling.
- UI should show base plus potion contribution, e.g. Power: 450 (+500).
