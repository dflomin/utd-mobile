# Monetization and Meta Progression

Allowed monetization:
- rewarded ad for 5 extra lives once per run
- cooldown-based optional ad for currency toward permanent upgrades
- cosmetic tower aesthetics around $0.99
- small non-greedy starter packs with currency/aesthetics

Avoid:
- gacha
- pay-to-win heroes/towers
- mandatory ads
- uncapped permanent-power ad grind

Meta progression:
- mostly cosmetic, horizontal, modest numerical growth
- account XP
- modest permanent upgrades
- cosmetic currency
- hero unlocks via milestones/account levels/challenges
