# Lives, Failure, Comeback

- Leaks reduce lives directly.
- Start target discussed around 30 HP/lives.
- Emergency tower reroll at thresholds such as 20, 10, 5 HP.
- One hero can restore HP with drawback.
- Super rare drop can heal HP.
- Rewarded ad may grant 5 extra lives once per run.
- Runs are endless but eventually doomed.
- Post-run summary should celebrate the run.
