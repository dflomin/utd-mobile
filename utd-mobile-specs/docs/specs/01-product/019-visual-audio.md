# Visual and Audio Direction

Visual:
- stylized semi-isometric/fake 2.5D, sprite-based, Phaser-friendly
- placeholder/cohesive art first
- replace epic towers/bosses/heroes/cosmetics over time
- moderate tower evolution by upgrade tier
- effect priority pool around 15-20 active visuals
- no floating damage numbers; HP bars show impact

Audio:
- minimal functional audio initially
- toggles for leak, new wave, wave finished, epic tower acquired
