# Items

- Items are build-defining or specialization-shaping modifiers.
- Each tower has 4 item slots.
- Items can be freely swapped.
- Items can add/modify effects.
- Item drops are highly random.
- Some builds may rely on certain items; not every build should always come together.
- Items have hardcoded config definitions and hardcoded effects.
- No randomized item stats/affixes for MVP.
- Item instances persist through equip/unequip.
