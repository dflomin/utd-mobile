# Heroes

- Heroes are selected before run.
- Heroes do not need physical map units.
- Heroes are archetype definers with modifiers/drawbacks.
- Initial launch target: around 10 heroes, ambitious but possible if disciplined.
- Heroes may affect gold, rerolls, drop rates, mana regen/max, minor range, etc.
- One hero can focus on HP recovery with drawback.
- One hero can modify rerolls: starting count, recharge frequency, upgraded reroll odds.
- Heroes unlock through milestones, account levels, and challenges.
