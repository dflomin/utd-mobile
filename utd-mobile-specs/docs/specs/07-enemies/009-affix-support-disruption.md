# Support and Disruption Affixes

Keep:
- Commander: buffs nearby enemies.
- Medic: heals nearby enemies.
- Banner Bearer: grants speed/HP style aura.
- Linked: shares damage among nearby enemies.
- Disruptor: periodically disables towers.
- Jammer: increases nearby tower cooldowns.
- Mana Leech: drains tower mana.
- Corrosive: towers attacking it receive temporary debuff.

Remove:
- Energized aura, enemies do not attack.
- Dark Nova, overlaps corrosive.
